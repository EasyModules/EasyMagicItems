## Runtime artwork

EasyMagicItems does not bundle item illustrations or character portraits. It displays actor portraits and item images supplied at runtime by the user’s installed Foundry VTT system and compendiums. Foundry-provided icons and Font Awesome classes are referenced but not redistributed by this package.
